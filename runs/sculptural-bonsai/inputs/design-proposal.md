# A more composed bonsai garden

**Proposal from a simulated interview. Not approved by the real user.**

## Recommended direction

Make the trees more sculptural and the surrounding garden quieter.

- **Give each tree a convincing silhouette.** Strengthen tapered trunks and their connections to asymmetric foliage. Keep open gaps. Preserve upright, leaning, sparse and cascading character; a sparse tree should not become full just to match its neighbors.
- **Use light to explain form.** Explore warm matte foliage, ceramic pots and fewer isolated bright flecks. Keep gold luminous and distinct from orange changes. Exact colors still need visual review.
- **Let the garden recede.** Reduce the visual weight of gravel lines and the existing brush circle. Keep plot positions stable and the inspector's space predictable.
- **Make conditions readable in words.** Test consistent project names and short factual conditions alongside existing markers. Show clearly whether a condition belongs to the project or a particular worktree. Give truncated names an accessible full-text reveal.
- **Keep useful controls visible.** Improve grouping and hierarchy before adding menus. Preserve readable text, target sizes, keyboard access and narrow-screen usability.

## Preserve what the trees mean

Gold reflects the current clean checkout condition, not a guarantee that GitHub checks passed. Ordinary changes and unchecked GitHub are not automatically urgent. Keep existing colors and non-color state markers, stable shape/history meaning, worktree saplings and overflow navigation. Keep the single-file runtime, embedded assets, dark brand, focus behavior and reduced-motion support.

## First comparison to build after approval

Show baseline and candidate specimens at actual board size: upright, leaning, sparse and cascading. Then compare quiet, crowded and attention-heavy boards with long names and worktrees, selected and unselected, including narrow screens. Check gold beside orange and verify that important conditions remain legible when another tree is selected.

Use existing regression tests and actual rendered review together. If labels cannot fit current plot bounds, surface the conflict instead of squeezing text or silently changing the bounds. Keep new visual test commands provisional until a real prototype exists.

## What still needs your judgment

The exact palette, label placement, acceptable amount of texture and final appearance remain open. Performance and accessibility improvements have not been measured.

Do not copy its invented progress captions, incorrect dashed-ring meaning, missing labels or unexplained gold/local-changes pairing. Its realistic detail is not a promise of what the procedural renderer can deliver.
